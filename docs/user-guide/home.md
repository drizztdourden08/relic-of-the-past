# The Home Screen

When you open a profile it lands on the Home screen. This is the hub for that profile: a quick look at what it is, the controls to start and stop the game, and all of its saves in one place.

## Profile at a glance

The top of the screen shows the profile's key details:

- The **ROM** it uses.
- When it was **last played**.
- When it was **created**.
- Its current **window mode**.

The title bar carries the play controls — **Play**, **Stop**, **Reset**, and **Pause** — so you can start or stop the game from anywhere in the profile.

## Saves

The Home screen gathers every kind of save the profile has.

### Hero save

The most recent named save, shown on its own card with its screenshot, so "continue where I left off" is one click away.

### Quick saves

Twelve quick save slots, also called save states. Each is a full snapshot you can drop and restore in an instant, with a screenshot and a timestamp, plus buttons to save (while the game runs) and load. See [Save States](save-states.md) for how saving and loading work.

### Named saves

Manual saves you give a name, so milestone points are easy to find later. While the game runs you can create a new one, and you can rename, overwrite, load, or delete it. A named save won't be overwritten by the quick-save shortcut.

### Auto-saves

Snapshots the game takes for you, each tagged with what triggered it, such as a timed interval or a room change. You load or delete them like any other save. Auto-save is configured in [Gameplay Settings](gameplay.md).

### Play sessions

A record of the profile's play sessions, with how long each ran and when.

## Settings

The rest of the profile page is its settings, grouped into tabs: [Display](display-graphics.md), [Visual Enhancements](visual-enhancements.md), [Audio & MSU](audio-msu.md), [Gameplay](gameplay.md), [HUD](hud.md), [Controls](input-controllers.md), and [Haptics](haptics.md). Every setting is saved with the profile.
