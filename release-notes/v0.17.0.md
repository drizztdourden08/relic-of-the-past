# Relic of the Past v0.17.0

Widescreen now holds up in the special overworld areas, and the world map markers sit where they belong.

## Special overworld areas

A few overworld locations are entered by stepping onto a switch tile instead of scrolling in from a neighbouring screen. The engine runs those through the same module it uses for falling into a dungeon, and almost nothing on the widescreen path recognised that, so they went wrong in several ways at once. All of this is fixed:

- The enhanced HUD is no longer invisible while you are in one of them.
- The view no longer drops back to 4:3 when you arrive, and no longer does it again every time you open the pause menu.
- Cheats and item grants work again while you are standing in one.
- The zoom transition in and out keeps the full widescreen frame. The side columns show real scenery instead of colour streaks, repeated slabs of map and torn vertical seams, and the mosaic squares stay square and aligned right across the frame.
- The coloured line that sat along the bottom of the map is gone.

## World map

- Map markers land in the right place in wide and tall views. They used to sit to the left of where they belong.

## HUD and menus

- Pause menu item names wrap and read correctly.
- The enhanced HUD shows the inventory properly.
- The cheat health control responds again.
