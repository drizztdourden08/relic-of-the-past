# Relic of the Past v0.20.0

A full item randomizer, a live music debugger, and one checks tracker component shared across the app.

## Randomizer

- A full item randomizer, generating seeds from a local port of Archipelago's own world logic, or connecting to an online room. A profile's seed and settings are frozen at creation.
- Two presets when creating a profile: Vanilla (4:3, Vanilla Safe on) and Enhanced (widescreen, the wishing pond, capacity upgrades, coloured rupees, and item sheen, all on).
- Bomb and arrow capacity, the magic meter, and the wallet can each be shuffled onto their own custom ladder, or sold at the wishing pond.
- The wishing pond can sell capacity levels, or hand out shuffled items at a price curve you choose.
- Dark rooms light up from carrying the lamp, fire rod, bombos medallion, or red cane.
- A quiver item, with its own sprite, replacing one shop's arrow slot, added to the item pool when randomized.
- Shops can charge in rupees, arrows, bombs, hearts, or bottle contents, with currency icons matching the HUD.
- Item sheen (the diagonal glint on a substituted item) plays on the hold-up receipt.
- World rupees animate their shine on the same cycle every rupee gem uses.
- A shop's shelf restocks after a multi-slot purchase.
- The cheat widget has a "max wallet" button alongside the rupee refill.

## Cheats

- A stats tab with one control per player property (health, magic, bombs, arrows, rupees, and their capacities), each with quarter-step buttons, generated from one table.
- A new cheat: Always Illuminate Dark Rooms.

## MSU Studio

- A live music debugger: every channel's state, authored odds against measured odds, and a feed of every sound raised, tagged by source.
- Replacement audio resets at the title screen: positions and ambient beds both clear.
- `.msul` packs register as a file type on Windows, with an icon and an "Open With" entry.

## Checks tracker

- One tracker component backs both the small Checks widget and the full tracker view. A randomizer profile shows what that seed placed.

## Fixes

- A sprite that ran off screen during a wide-view sweep stays off screen.
