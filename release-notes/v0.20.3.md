# Relic of the Past v0.20.3

Playtest fixes for randomizer softlocks, dark rooms and world item art, plus a prefilled file name.

## Randomizer

- Shops no longer lock a required upgrade behind its own currency, so a seed cannot ask you to buy bombs with bombs.
- Dark rooms light from the items you have ticked, including when a light is not required for the room.
- Old Man, Spectacle Rock, Sunken Treasure, Dark Maze and six other checks now grant the right item and mark themselves complete.
- The cheat console can grant from a check and mark it complete through the game's own chest path.
- The pedestal item sits centred on the sword hole, and capacity upgrades show their icon on world items.
- Coloured rupees on a shop shelf draw the whole gem. A 50, 100 or 300 rupee no longer shows as a sliver.
- Fixed the falling rupee art, the mirror warp tile corruption and the crash on leaving a boss room.

## Navigation

- The Lost Woods crossings no longer flash orange, and scroll crossings no longer show stale edges.

## Quality of life

- Creating a new file lands on the naming screen already named Link, with the cursor on End, so one press confirms it. Type over it for any other name.
- New Prefill File Name setting under Gameplay, Quality of Life. On for the Enhanced preset, off by default and for Vanilla.
