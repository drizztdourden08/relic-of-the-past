# Relic of the Past v0.15.0

A controllers release.

## Controllers

- All controller input now runs through SDL3 on every platform. The old input layer is gone.
- Controllers are identified against the community mapping database SDL uses.
- Each controller card shows what the pad supports:
  - how it is connected
  - rumble
  - motion sensors
- Rumble strength is adjustable per controller. Turn a weak pad up, or a harsh one down.

## Reporting a controller that doesn't work

- Every controller card has a "Report this controller as not working" option. It records what your pad reports and files it for you. You review everything before it is sent.
- The guided run:
  - reads your controller's layout
  - asks you to press each button and move each stick, showing you live what it sees
  - takes a resting reading to measure stick drift
