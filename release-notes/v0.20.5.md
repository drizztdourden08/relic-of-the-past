# Relic of the Past v0.20.5

Randomizer settings for every fairy pond, text speed controls with a new message box, and a cheat widget built on the game's own HUD.

## Randomizer

- The Waterfall Fairy and the Pyramid Fairy each get their own settings tab, like the Hylia Fairy.
- Every fairy has three modes. Vanilla grants keeps the original upgrades. Vanilla cost hands out two pool items, one per visit. Custom sells a ladder of pool items over a set number of throws.
- In Custom, a fairy can ask for rupees, bombs, arrows, bottles or an item you hold up and get back. She names the price in a yes or no box, and saying no or being unable to pay costs nothing.
- The asks come from the seed, so the settings preview shows the real sequence before you generate.
- The chest in Link's house counts as reachable from the start, since the game opens in that house.

## Dialog

- New Dialog section under Gameplay. A text speed slider goes from the game's pace to instant, holding A types faster, B finishes the page, and a letter by letter mode reads one letter at a time. The telepathy and skip options moved here too.
- New Dialog Box section under HUD draws an enhanced box over every message: talk boxes, telepathy, the story crawl and the save, spawn and game over menus.
- Pick the original glyphs or a modern font, then the ink, border, background, opacity and an animated pattern. Button prompts show the controls you mapped.
- The telepathy background is two settings now. Behind Intro Telepathy covers Zelda's first call while Link is in bed and is off by default, so the opening stays dark. Behind Telepathy covers every other call and is on by default.
- The Enhanced preset starts with the full dialog setup.

## Cheats

- The cheat widget is rebuilt around the game's HUD and pause menu. Click the life meter, magic meter, counters or bottles to set a value, and scroll to step it.
- The Items tab is the pause menu made clickable. You can remove items now, not only give them. Pendants, crystals, heart pieces, maps, compasses and big keys toggle in place.
- Maximums follow the file: the game's own tiers on a vanilla file, and the upgrades the seed can reach on a randomized one.
- Checks moved to a tab of their own, next to a new Rules tab for walking through walls, dark room light and damage.

## Fixes

- Restarting the game from the app stops the old core. It used to keep running in the background, and with replacement music on, its title theme cut into the live game every few seconds.
- Black Background is now Hide Space Beyond Walls. It blacks out the space past the walls of houses, caves and the sanctuary, and no longer erases tree tops, bushes, the sanctuary carpet or raised cave floors. Dungeons are left alone.
- Falling into an overworld hole keeps the wide view instead of snapping to 4:3 for the fall. It follows the Smooth Transitions setting.
- Archery Game Asks For A Bow moved from Graphics to Gameplay.
