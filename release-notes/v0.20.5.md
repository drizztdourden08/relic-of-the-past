# Relic of the Past v0.20.5

Randomizer settings for every fairy pond, text speed controls, an enhanced message box and a rebuilt cheat widget.

## Randomizer

- Waterfall Fairy and Pyramid Fairy settings tabs, matching the Hylia Fairy.
- Three fairy modes: Vanilla grants, Vanilla cost (two pool items, one per visit) and Custom (a ladder of pool items over a set number of throws).
- Custom fairy asks for rupees, bombs, arrows, bottles or a held item, shown in a yes or no box. Declining or being unable to pay costs nothing.
- The chest in Link's house is reachable from the start in logic.
- Stumpy gives his check in seeds where the shovel or flute comes first, and gives it only once.

## Dialog

- Dialog section under Gameplay: text speed slider from the original pace to instant, hold A to type faster, B to finish the page, and a letter by letter mode. The telepathy and skip options live here too.
- Dialog Box section under HUD: an enhanced box for talk boxes, telepathy, the story crawl and the save, spawn and game over menus.
- Message box options for original glyphs or a modern font, ink, border, background, opacity and an animated pattern.
- Button prompts in messages show the mapped controls.
- Separate telepathy background settings. Behind Intro Telepathy covers Zelda's first call and is off by default. Behind Telepathy covers every other call and is on by default.
- Full dialog setup in the Enhanced preset.

## Cheats

- Cheat widget rebuilt around the game's HUD and pause menu. The life meter, magic meter, counters and bottles set values on click and step on scroll.
- Items tab built from the pause menu, with item removal alongside giving. Pendants, crystals, heart pieces, maps, compasses and big keys toggle in place.
- Maximums follow the file: the original tiers on a vanilla file, the seed's reachable upgrades on a randomized one.
- Checks tab, and a Rules tab for walking through walls, dark room light and damage.

## Fixes

- Restarting the game stops the old core. It kept running in the background and cut its title music into the live game.
- Black Background renamed to Hide Space Beyond Walls. It covers the space past the walls of houses, caves and the sanctuary, and no longer erases tree tops, bushes, the sanctuary carpet or raised cave floors.
- The wide view holds through a fall into an overworld hole, following the Smooth Transitions setting.
- Archery Game Asks For A Bow moved from Graphics to Gameplay.
