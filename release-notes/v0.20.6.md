# Relic of the Past v0.20.6

The wide and tall views hold through the sequences that used to snap back to 4:3, and kill rooms open again.

## View

- The death sequence keeps the view. The closing circle, the red flash, GAME OVER and the save menu all take the full width, and the scene no longer jumps when the circle starts.
- Spotlight crossings, falling down a hole, boss victory, save and quit, mirror warp, Ganon and the triforce room keep the view.
- The iris opens onto the whole frame, on a curve that starts slow and finishes fast.
- Copy, erase and name take the full width. The intro, the title and the file screens fill their margins with their own background.
- Each of the opening story's five scenes measures its own frame. Its sprites stay in the picture, the escort walks to where the view ends, and the title stops leaking copies of itself as the story starts.
- Projectiles are drawn and culled for the rows a tall view adds.
- The space beyond indoor walls follows the room's tileset instead of its palace.
- The dam's flood stops filling the band above the water.
- The edge effect gained a top band, and fades at its edge instead of cutting.
- The enhanced HUD sits at the top in a tall view, as it already did in a wide one.

## Settings

- One Widescreen Fixes switch on Display, on by default with extended rendering. A tall view counts as extended, which it did not before.

## Fixes

- Kill rooms whose doors stayed shut after every visible enemy was dead, seen in the Tower of Hera and Agahnim's Tower. An enemy standing past the wall of the room section no longer counts against the room being clear.
- A dark room lit by the lamp keeps the death circle at the original width, so the lamp cone cannot leak into the sides.
