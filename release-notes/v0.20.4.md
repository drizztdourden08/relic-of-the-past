# Relic of the Past v0.20.4

Turbo returns, the map marker keeps custom sprite colours, and the settings nav drops the empty subsections.

## Pacing

- Turbo is back. Hold the rebindable Turbo shortcut, from keyboard or pad, and the game runs fast while you hold it.
- New Turbo Speed ladder from 1.25x to 10x, default 2x. Both sit under Gameplay, in a new Game Flow section that also holds the save state options.

## Fixes

- The head marker on both map screens takes the colours of a custom player sprite.
- Closing a map keeps the bunny colours right, with a new bug fix toggle behind it. A custom sheet re-lands its palette for the form you are in, including after a save state load.

## Settings

- Nineteen sections lost the subsection that existed only to hold their list, so the side nav shows the real structure.
- A section heading is a jump target of its own now, so a flat section is reachable without a child entry.
- Audio's five volume sliders read as one list.
