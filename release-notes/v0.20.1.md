# Relic of the Past v0.20.1

Debug report tooling for contributors, plus a batch of randomizer fixes that unblock seeds stuck at zero available checks.

## Debug reports

- A new Contributor-tab toggle enables a titlebar capture recorder (Tab by default, rebindable) and a floating "send debug report" button over the gameplay area.
- Recording samples a screenshot and the nav state several times a second and encodes it to video on stop, keeping a full local copy alongside a smaller version sized for upload.
- A report packages the current save, your most recent saves, settings, randomizer config, and profile/input config, built locally before the issue form opens. The zip uploads once you file the issue.

## Randomizer

- The wishing pond's two counter checks are wired up, so the pond contributes locations to a seed, and its "In Pool" preview total matches the Checks widget.
- 7 previously undetected pickups now count toward a seed's total: Peg Cave, Bumper Cave Ledge, Brewery, C-Shaped House, Chest Game, and both Pyramid Fairy grants.
- The Checks widget's total reflects what a seed actually generated, shop slots included.
- Tracker availability rebuilds from every seed setting. Dark rooms lit by the fire rod or cane of Somaria, and shop purchases from an earlier session, now count correctly toward a seed's available checks.
- Replaced the quiver's held-item sprite with one provided by efracrafting.
