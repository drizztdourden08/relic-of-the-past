# Relic of the Past v0.19.1

A Linux fix on top of v0.19.0: the AppImage starts on older distributions again.

## Linux

- On distributions older than Ubuntu 24.04 (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 and the like), the AppImage opened an empty window. The updater's native component needs a newer system library than those distributions ship, and it stopped the app from starting.
- The app now starts there without automatic updates. Check the releases page for new versions.
- Nothing changes on Windows, or on newer Linux distributions.
