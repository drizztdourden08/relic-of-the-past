# Relic of the Past v0.18.0

Every setting that touches the game core can now be switched off on its own, widescreen sprite placement is fixed, and the updater warns you before an update would break your saves.

## Settings and Vanilla Safe

- Every setting that touches the game core now has its own on/off switch — 79 of them, up from 34. Turning off navigation data, for example, no longer also turns off the tracker.
- Vanilla Safe, which forces all of that off at once, now actually locks every setting it disables (58 of 85) instead of leaving some of them free to be turned back on.
- A widget left empty by a setting now shows a message explaining why, instead of sitting there blank.
- Cheats moved into Gameplay settings with their own panel, and no longer come with default key bindings.
- Fixed: the HUD showing doubled up, a custom sprite not reverting once its setting was turned off, cheats not surviving a save and reload, and some settings silently failing to take effect after loading a save until you touched them again.

## Wide and tall views

Every aspect of the widescreen and "tallscreen" support has been reviewed, tested and made sure there are no "leaks" anywhere outside of the vanilla 4:3 resolution. A lot of small bugs and leaks have been caught and fixed.

Here's some specific:
- Sprites near the very top or bottom of a tall view no longer vanish for a single frame as they cross it.
- Sprites meant to stay off-screen above the play area no longer draw partway down into it — this could affect visible items such as the pedestal sword.
- The HUD and message text no longer bleed stray glyphs into the extra vertical space in a tall view.
- The camera now follows the player rather than snapping to the frame's centre, so it only leans toward an edge when the view is actually clamped there.
- Two new settings: an extended play area, and what happens off-screen (paused, idle, or still acting).

## Updates

- Before installing an update, the app now checks whether that update writes save states in a format the current app can read, and warns you if it doesn't.
