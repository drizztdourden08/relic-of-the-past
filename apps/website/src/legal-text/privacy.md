# Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Relic of the Past website, the desktop app, Sanctuary and the Hookshop. It is issued by The Relic of the Past maintainers.

The desktop app collects nothing. Data is only kept when you sign in to Sanctuary or the Hookshop, send a bug report, or share a file or a pack.

## The website

The website stores nothing about you and sets no cookies. Your browser asks GitHub's public API for the repository numbers and the latest release, so GitHub sees that request. The page also loads its heading font from Google Fonts, so Google sees that request. GitHub Pages hosts the site and keeps its own server logs.

## The desktop app

There is no analytics and no tracking. Your ROM, saves and profiles stay on your computer. The app goes online only to check GitHub Releases for updates, to send a bug report when you ask it to, and to talk to the Hookshop or an Archipelago room when you use them.

## Signing in

Sanctuary and the Hookshop let you sign in with Discord, GitHub or Google. One account can link all three. Each provider tells us the following.

- Discord: your ID, username, avatar, email, and your roles on our Discord server. We use them for your account and for access groups.
- GitHub: your ID, login, avatar and email. We use them for your account and for contributor access.
- Google: your ID, name, picture and email. We use them for your account.

We never receive your password for any of these providers. We ask each provider only for the access it needs: Discord identify, email and guilds.members.read, GitHub read:user and user:email, and Google openid, email and profile.

Signing in sets a session cookie that lasts 30 days. Your account is kept until you ask us to delete it.

## Bug reports

A bug report holds the text you write, the save file and debug capture you choose to attach, and a contact email when you are not signed in. Only administrators see that email.

Each report opens an issue on the public GitHub repository with your display name. The issue text is public. Attached files are visible to the maintainers only.

We keep a report for 30 days after its issue closes, and for 90 days at most if the issue never closes.

## Sanctuary files

When you share a file in Sanctuary we keep the file, its versions and your group rights. We keep them until you or a reviewer remove them. Members of the groups you share with can see them.

## Hookshop packs

When you submit a pack we keep the pack files, pictures, versions and reviews. We keep them until you withdraw the pack. Once a pack is approved it is public, under the licence you pick.

## Who else sees your data

The provider you sign in with sees that you signed in. GitHub sees website requests, update checks and public issues. Google Fonts sees the font request from the website. We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

## Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account, your files and your packs, or to remove a bug report, by writing to the address below. Removing a public GitHub issue is done on GitHub.

## Children

You must be 13 or older to sign in, which is Discord's own minimum age. We do not knowingly keep data from anyone younger.

## Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. The history is public in the repository.
