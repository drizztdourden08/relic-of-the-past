# Relic of the Past

Relic of the Past is a desktop port of *The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past* for Windows, macOS and Linux. It runs a decompiled C reimplementation of the game in WebAssembly inside an Electron app, with save profiles, controller support, MSU-1 audio and randomizer tools. No Nintendo game files are included: the app needs your own legally obtained ROM.

## In an Archipelia multiworld

- Create a profile in Archipelia mode and join a lobby on this computer, on your local network or on a remote host, by its code or address.
- The randomizer options are the world's options, and the seed you play is the one the multiworld generated.
- Items found in your game are sent to the other players, and items sent to you arrive in your game.
- DeathLink is available as a randomizer option.

## Archipelago versions

The world is published for Archipelago 0.6.6, 0.6.7 and 0.6.8. A host and its players must run the same Archipelago version.
